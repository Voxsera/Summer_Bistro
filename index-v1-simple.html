Our story

Made for night owls & early birds

Summer Bistro started with a simple idea: a great café shouldn't have closing time. Whether you're wrapping up a late shift, catching up with friends after a movie, or grabbing breakfast before the city wakes up — our doors, and our espresso machine, are always on.

Tucked under the metro on Road No. 5, Jubilee Hills, we built a little green escape — a garden with a waterfall, cosy lounge corners and a kitchen that serves continental and fusion favourites at every hour.
